Delorun studio gives Delorun its look and its everyday chat tools.

## What it adds

- The Delorun look: colours, typefaces and spacing, with the Appearance settings.
- Settings grouped by topic, with search.
- Snooze and settle for chats, each with its own shelf.
- The chat list as cards, with workspace icons and header art.
- The first-run guide, which ends in a new chat.
- Two extras that stay off until you turn them on: the cost meter, which shows the estimated cost of a chat under the message box, and the Thinking tab, which shows the model's reasoning word by word in the right sidebar.

## Turn an extra on

Switch the cost meter or the Thinking tab on in the Components list on this page.

## Go back to the stock look

Settings → General → Look has one switch back to the stock look. Snoozed and settled chats stay stored, and they return when you turn the Delorun look on again.
