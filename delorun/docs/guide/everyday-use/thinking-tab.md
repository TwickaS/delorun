# Read the model's thinking word by word

English | [中文](thinking-tab.zh.md)

## Summary

This page shows how to read the model's reasoning and reply one word at a time, in the right sidebar, while the model writes them. It helps when you want to follow along without scrolling a long answer. You need Delorun running with a model provider ([Model providers](../get-started/providers.md)). The tab is off until you turn it on, and it makes no request of its own: it shows what the model already sent to the [chat](../../reference/glossary.md#chat).

## Turn it on

Open Plugins in the sidebar and open **Delorun studio**. Under Components, find **Thinking tab** and turn it on; it starts at once, with no restart. Then open the right sidebar and pick **Thinking**. The tab follows the latest turn of the chat you are in.

The first-run guide's Recommended plugins step offers the same switch.

## The band and the page

The band shows one word at a time, in a fixed place, with the letter your eye fixes on marked. A word that ends a sentence or a clause stays a little longer.

Below the band, the page lists every word. What you have read is above the band and what you have not read yet is below it, each part labelled Reasoning or Reply. Scroll either side to look back or ahead; the band keeps its place.

## Choose what and how fast

- **Read** picks the reasoning, the reply, or both in the order the model wrote them.
- **Pace** picks how the band moves: **Live** shows each word as the model writes it, **Set rate** plays at 100 to 1,000 words a minute, and **Model's rate** follows how fast the model is writing.
- **Delay** holds each word back for up to 30 seconds after it was written, so you read a steady stream behind the model. It applies to a set rate and to the model's rate.
- The controls play and pause, step one word back or forward, start again, and skip to the newest word.

Your choices stay in this browser.

<details>
<summary>Edge cases</summary>

- If your system asks for reduced motion, then the tab starts paused.
- If the text is Chinese or Japanese, which has no spaces between words, then the tab still reads it word by word.
- If the chat has no reasoning or reply yet, then the tab says there is nothing to read.
- If you switch to the stock look in Settings, then the tab disappears. Turn the Delorun look back on to see it again.
- If you turn the plugin off, then the tab goes and your chat keeps its history.

</details>
