- An approved phone can add and test model keys; stored keys are never shown, not even their first or last characters or their length, and a phone can name only the keys that model providers use.
- An approved phone can change everyday settings (appearance, accent, studio look, language, sidebar, onboarding progress) and turn on Delorun's own optional plugins, with no unlock; installing or removing plugins stays at the computer.
