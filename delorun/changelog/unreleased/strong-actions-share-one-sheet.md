- Every sensitive action on a phone now confirms in one bottom sheet that shows the action's own icon and title, the workspace and chat it belongs to when known, what it allows as you and how long it lasts. Confirm is a full-width button with the Face ID icon inside it, or "Confirm with PIN" on a phone with no passkey, which opens the keypad inside the same sheet. Cancel is a plain button, and nothing has a focus ring when the sheet opens.
