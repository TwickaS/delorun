---
area: Safety and privacy
section: Changed
---

- With protection off, the agent's plugin tool works as it does in stock DeepSeek Harness once you give its single full-access approval: it installs, removes, turns on, configures, undoes and restores plugins, and plugins it installs or links reload when it saves them. Each of these is listed in Changes as a change that loaded without review, and you can undo it there or with `delorun changes undo`. With protection on, nothing changes: installs wait for your review, and turning a plugin on, undoing and restoring are left to you. A protection setting that cannot be read keeps every action held, as with protection on.
