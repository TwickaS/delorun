- Multiview has a layout picker with a small picture of each layout: Auto, Main and stack, Side by side, Stacked rows, Grid, Main on top, Spiral, Centred main, Activity mosaic and Tabs. Auto is the new default: up to three panes side by side, then rows on a tall window, a centred main chat on a very wide one and the main chat beside a stack of agents otherwise. A pane that would be smaller than the least pane size becomes a tab of its neighbour instead of being hidden, and Auto never rearranges while you drag or type.
- Main and stack puts the main chat on the left or the right, stacks the agents as rows, columns, a grid or a spiral, and remembers the main chat's share when you drag the divider. Activity mosaic gives working agents the large places and finished ones small places, with a short glide that reduced motion turns off.
- Arranging the panes yourself saves the arrangement as Yours, which the next Multiview uses with its own agents; a message offers Undo, and Reset to Auto clears it. Choosing a layout in the picker also makes it your default. Settings, Multiview, a new page, chooses whether your arrangement is remembered for the whole studio, one workspace or one chat, whether resizing panes saves it or only moving them does, and holds the other Multiview settings, including the space between panes, from edge to edge up to 32 pixels.
- New keyboard commands, with no keys until you give them some, choose the next or previous layout, move focus between panes, swap a pane with the main chat, and grow or shrink the main chat.
