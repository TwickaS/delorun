- On a phone or other device, settings that used to disappear now show what they can: the PIN mode section shows whether PIN mode is on and says plainly that setting it is done on your computer, and why. Where an action needs an unlock, the notice states how many minutes the unlock lasts.
- Setting up Delorun from a phone now says plainly which step only your computer can do and why. For a PIN, "Do it on my computer" keeps the step for you, and the studio on your computer shows a quiet "Finish setup · N left" in its sidebar that opens just those steps and goes away when none are left.
