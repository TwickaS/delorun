---
area: Safety and privacy
section: Removed
---

- Phone powers, with its Careful and Full levels, is gone. A phone no longer has a level to choose: each action that changes what Delorun runs, such as opening a terminal, applying a change, turning protection off, making your copy of Delorun, restarting it and updating it, asks for Face ID, or for your PIN on a phone with no Face ID. With Face ID, one confirmation can cover the next few minutes of strong actions on that phone (Settings, Face ID, Ask again after; Every time asks for each one); the PIN always covers one action. A phone without Face ID can now do these strong actions, and installs, full access, network settings, MCP server and external agent answers, the folder picker, new workspaces, settings changes, saving or removing a model key and approval answers too, by typing its PIN once for each action: the PIN confirms that one action and opens no window, so the next action asks again, where Careful refused them before. A phone with Face ID confirms with Face ID, and that Face ID lets strong actions on that phone go without asking again for the time Ask again after names, 15 minutes unless you choose otherwise, except adding or removing devices and passkeys, the Face ID settings, turning protection off, a workspace's full access and remote access, which ask every time. No PIN unlock, code from your computer, approval grace or Never ask mode opens any of them, so a phone that was unlocked before still asks, and a refused action no longer asks for a code from your computer. Typing your PIN no longer unlocks a phone's page for a while: the PIN lock screen is gone, every phone is refused a PIN unlock, and a phone with no Face ID reaches exactly what a phone with Face ID reaches. A phone's Settings, Devices no longer offers Unlock, Show the code, Lock now or Add a device (it says to add devices at your computer), your computer's Settings, Devices shows an older unlock request only to decline it, `delorun reach on` and setup no longer say a phone cannot grant full access, PIN mode drops its idle time and lock-when-hidden choices (`delorun devices pin options` says they have no effect), and on a phone the first-run guide says model keys are tested at your computer instead of offering a Try again that always fails. A phone with no Face ID on a home with no PIN is told how to set up Face ID, or to set a PIN at your computer, instead of being shown a keypad that cannot work. `delorun devices powers` and `delorun setup --phone-powers` no longer exist, setup stops asking what an unlocked phone may do, the Settings, Devices choice is removed, and the `phonePowersOnUpgrade` setting of the device sign-in plugin and the `/auth/phone-powers` address are gone. An answers file that still holds `phonePowers` loads, and the answer is ignored with a line saying so.
