- The setup guide keeps your current workspace when you skip the Workspace step after running setup again, and Continue adds the folder you highlighted instead of leaving two filled buttons on screen.
- Escape works as Finish later, and a "Finish setup" chip above the message box brings the guide back at the step you left. Ready no longer offers Finish later.
- Recommended plugins show placeholder rows until their titles arrive, and Agents asks "Add Claude Code first?" instead of dropping an open Add question when you press Continue.
- Phone access shows its command in a code box with "Not now" in place of Skip, the Ready list names the colour mode, and the Look preview stays in view while you choose.
- The "Finish setup" chip now shows after Finish later even before your first chat exists, and Skip on Workspace keeps your workspace when you have one and uses the default when you have none.
- Phone access: "I've done this" continues once you have run the command, and Ready says whether phone access is on, not set up, or worth checking in Settings → Devices.
