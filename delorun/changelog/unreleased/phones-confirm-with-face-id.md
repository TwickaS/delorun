- Phones confirm sensitive actions with Face ID, or with the device lock where there is no Face ID, and set it up when pairing or in Settings, Devices. It is on for every home, with no preview switch to turn on; a home that had set the old `unlock.passkeys` preview switch keeps its other preview switches, and the retired one is ignored with a line in the log. A phone with no passkey confirms with its PIN. Each sensitive action you take on a phone asks once, at the moment you take it, in plain words such as "Install a plugin", "Open a terminal" or "Save a model key": installing, updating, switching or removing plugins; full access, network settings, MCP server and external agent answers and approval answers; a terminal you open or look at, which asks once and again only after its confirmation lapsed (five minutes without a new terminal call, or an hour); a reload or a second tab on the same phone showing that terminal does not ask before then, and another terminal asks for itself; saved terminal tabs you are not looking at never ask, and a phone does not keep them alive, so the computer closes an idle one after two hours, as it does any idle terminal no window holds; the folder picker, which asks once while it stays open; new workspaces and their defaults, a chat in a folder outside your workspaces, slash commands, settings changes, saving or removing a model key, deleting from the trash forever, the details of a problem report, checking an external agent's plan limits now, and Changes and updates. One sheet shows at a time, and the next waits until you answer it. Cancel sends nothing, and the screen shows that the action did not go through. Some things stay at your computer and never ask on a phone: model-written plugins, the list of an agent's terminals in a chat's header, and browsing with your own browser while you are away. In the first minutes after a phone is paired, a change to a consent or guard plugin's settings waits until that setup window ends. Your computer never asks. Phone powers is gone, so there is no level to choose.
