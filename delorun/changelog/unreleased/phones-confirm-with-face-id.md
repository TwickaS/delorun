- Phones confirm sensitive actions with Face ID, or with the device lock where there is no Face ID, and set it up when pairing or in Settings, Devices. It is on for every home, with no preview switch to turn on; a home that had set the old `unlock.passkeys` preview switch keeps its other preview switches, and the retired one is ignored with a line in the log. A phone with no passkey confirms with its PIN. Each sensitive action you take on a phone asks once, at the moment you take it, in plain words such as "Install a plugin", "Open a terminal" or "Save a model key": installing, updating, switching or removing plugins; full access, network settings, MCP server and external agent answers and approval answers; opening a terminal, which asks once when it opens and once when a reloaded page reopens it; the folder picker, which asks once while it stays open; the list of MCP servers, which asks once when it opens; new workspaces and their defaults, a chat in a folder outside your workspaces, slash commands, settings changes, saving or removing a model key, deleting from the trash forever, the details of a problem report, and Changes and updates. One sheet shows at a time, and the next waits until you answer it. Cancel sends nothing, and the screen shows that the action did not go through. Some things stay at your computer and never ask on a phone: model-written plugins, the list of an agent's terminals in a chat's header, and browsing with your own browser while you are away. In the first minutes after a phone is paired, a change to a consent or guard plugin's settings waits until that setup window ends. Your computer never asks. Phone powers is gone, so there is no level to choose.
