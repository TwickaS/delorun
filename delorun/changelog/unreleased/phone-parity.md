- A paired phone now does what the computer's studio does. Reading and tightening work without an unlock; changes that widen access (accepting an MCP server, allowing an agent into a workspace, a workspace's full access or network) work after an unlock, with a code from the computer or your PIN, whether or not dangerous mode is on. A locked phone is told to unlock first. Approving devices, setting the PIN and turning remote access on or off stay at the computer.
