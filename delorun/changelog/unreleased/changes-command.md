- `delorun changes` lists the changes to your plugins, those that loaded without review and those that wait for your review, and shows each with its files, a diff and a summary of what it can do. `apply`, `discard` and `undo` decide about one change after you confirm in a terminal, with a second confirmation when the change runs commands, opens network connections or does anything else the summary names. A running Delorun loads an applied change at once and goes back to what ran before when it fails to load. `off` and `on` turn one plugin off and on again, and `protection on` makes changes wait for your review, which `protection off`, the default, ends; each says what it means, asks, and applies without a restart. `work <plugin> [--for <minutes>]` loads every edit you save in a plugin's folder live for up to two hours, or the minutes you give up to eight hours, going back to the last version that loaded when an edit fails to load. `done <plugin>` shows the folder's version with its files and diff and approves exactly that version as one change; a session that ends without you, when Delorun stops or its time is up, approves nothing and leaves the folder's content waiting for your review. `delorun status` names an open work session and when it ends. With protection on, a plugin you link with `delorun plugin --profile default add link:<folder>`, or bring back with a restore or undo, loads nothing from its folder until you apply its content, also while Delorun runs; the approved copy includes the plugin's installed packages; and a plugin folder Delorun cannot read no longer stops Delorun from starting. When Delorun fails to start right after you applied a change to a plugin or turned one on, it starts once more without that change, names it with `delorun changes apply <id>` and `undo <id>` at every start, in `delorun changes list` and in `delorun doctor`, and rewrites none of your files; when it fails again it points to `delorun recover safe-mode`.
