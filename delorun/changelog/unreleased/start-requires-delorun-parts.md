area: Safety and privacy
section: Security
---

- `delorun` refuses to start when your command-line profile cannot load Delorun's own bundle, and prints how to fix it, instead of starting with DeepSeek Harness's defaults, which upload session logs to DeepSeek.
- `delorun` stops and tells you how to fix it when one of Delorun's own plugins, such as the Privacy section in Settings, fails to load, instead of running without it.
