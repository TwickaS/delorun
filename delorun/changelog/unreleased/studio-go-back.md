- After an update, Settings → Updates offers **Go back…**, which returns to the release before it. It says that chats made since the update are set aside, not deleted, and can be removed later in Settings → Trash; when the update took no copy of your chats it offers **Go back, settings only…** and your chats stay as they are. You choose whether running chats are stopped now, and Delorun restarts on the earlier release. It needs the computer; a phone confirms with Face ID or its PIN first, and where a device may not do it the button reads "Update at your computer".
