# Delorun 0.1.0-rc.4

Delorun 0.1.0-rc.4 is the fourth release candidate of Delorun, the community fork of DeepSeek Harness. It is based on DeepSeek Harness `dsh-v0.2.2-rc.3`, as rc.3 was. It carries every change of rc.3, which could not be installed over rc.2, and fixes the update problem behind that.

## Highlights

- **Updates you can read first.** When a new release switches something off through its own settings, the update now lists it and asks you before applying, and its health check still refuses anything switched off without being listed. 0.1.0-rc.3 could not update from rc.2 for this reason; rc.4 updates from both.
- **Everything from rc.3.** The sidebar update notice and the Settings switch for the release check, a trust badge that says what was checked at start, and a first setup step without the DeepSeek Account provider.

## Platforms

Delorun 0.1.0-rc.4 runs on Linux (x64 and arm64) and on Windows through WSL2, as rc.3 did.

## Upgrading from 0.1.0-rc.2 or 0.1.0-rc.3

Run `delorun update`. From rc.2, the studio cannot show the update notice, so the update is announced in the terminal and by `delorun update --check`; from rc.4 on, the sidebar shows the next one. Your chats, workspaces, settings, plugins and approved devices carry over, and nothing needs changing by hand. If the new version fails its health check, Delorun goes back on its own, and `delorun recover rollback` takes you back at any time.

## Install and update
- **Fixed:** An update now lists every row the new release switches off through its own settings, and asks you before it applies them. Before, such a row was left out of the list, so the health check refused the switched-off row after the update started and rolled the update back. A row switched off without being listed still fails the check.
