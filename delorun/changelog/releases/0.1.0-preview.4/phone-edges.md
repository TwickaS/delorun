- On an iPhone the studio's colour reaches the screen's edges: the status bar, the home indicator and the area beside Safari's bars take the colour of the surface at that edge, so the sidebar's header gradient, a chat, Settings and a dialog each continue into the system chrome with no dark band or seam. - The studio fills the screen as an installed app: the sidebar's header and foot and the chat's composer reach the physical edges, and their content stays clear of the notch and the home indicator in both orientations.
- The studio no longer bounces when a list is dragged past its end, and the composer stays above the on-screen keyboard while typing.
- The installed app launches on the studio's own dark surface instead of a white flash, and its home-screen name is Delorun.
