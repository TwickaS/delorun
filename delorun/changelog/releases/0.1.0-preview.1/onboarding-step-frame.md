- Setup keeps the step name and "Step n of 7" with a progress bar at the top, and Skip, Back and Continue at the bottom, at every window width; only the middle scrolls. Errors and warnings show in a full-width line under the card with what to do next, and a failed plugin check shows a note at the top of the step.
