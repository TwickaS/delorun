- Delorun is an agent harness that you shape to your own work: the themes, the sidebar, the plugins, the modes, even Delorun itself. It is built on DeepSeek Harness, runs its plugins and imports your chats, and adds a studio you open in a browser, a guided first run, undo for every change to your setup, protections for what agents may do, and reaching your home from your phone. This first public version, 0.1.0-preview.2.1, is described below: everything that differs from stock DeepSeek Harness, grouped by where you meet it.
