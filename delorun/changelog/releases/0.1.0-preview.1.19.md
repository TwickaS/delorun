# Delorun 0.1.0-preview.1.19

## Install and update
- While an update waits for running chats, its alert in the sidebar has a ⋯ menu with Stop running chats and update. A confirm step names each chat that will stop and says whether it will continue after the update, then Delorun asks for your confirmation as it does for starting an update, stops the chats (keeping their work so far) and goes on. By default the updated Delorun sends each stopped chat one message to carry on; Settings → Updates has a switch, Continue stopped chats after an update, and with it off each stopped chat shows Stopped for the update with a Continue button.
- While the connection is lost, Delorun is reconnecting, or an update waits or switches, the state shows as a compact alert in the sidebar until it ends, with its Cancel update button; on a phone it also shows as a toast at the top that you can close. After an update, the outcome card shows up to five one-line highlights from the release notes of every version the update skipped, with a Read more button that opens the full change log of those versions in a sheet (full screen on a phone). It reads the notes the update shipped, so it works offline. The card keeps each version number on one line and shows the finish time in your own time zone.
- **Fixed:** Settings → Updates opens with its content again. It showed an empty panel, with no title, because the page failed to read its Continue stopped chats switch.

## Studio
- Browser now has its own page in Settings. It is on unless you turn it off: a home that never chose adds the Browser plugin when the page or the setup guide opens, and a home that chose off, here or in Plugins, stays off. The page shows whether Browser is ready in plain words, downloads the browser it needs, tests it by opening a page and showing a picture of it, and shows where your own Chrome stands.
- The "Choose a folder" step of New workspace uses the whole dialog. One row holds clickable breadcrumbs from Home, the folder search and a New folder icon button. The folders fill the width until you select one; then a summary of it opens beside them: its name and path, the stack its project files show (Node.js, Python, Rust and others), its Git branch and latest commit, its top-level files with their sizes, and a Use this folder button. On a phone the folders fill the screen, and the summary opens as its own full-screen step with a Back button.
- Short messages, the downloads card and the update progress card now share one place on the screen: bottom left on a computer or tablet, with the newest at the bottom, older ones pushed up and at most three showing, and full width at the top of a phone. Settings → Appearance → Toast position moves them to any corner or to the top or bottom centre.
- **Fixed:** The setup guide reads better on a phone. An odd last card fills the row instead of standing alone at half width, and the Welcome preview is as tall as its content. On Models, the whole row is the tick target with a larger circle, and the description wraps with a full-size More under it. On Agents, the icon lines up with the title, "sign-in" stays on one line and the detail of what an agent can do sits under a disclosure. The Ready summary shows the folder name with its path under it, which breaks only after a slash. The Browser step no longer names its package. The collapsed sidebar's Finish setup icon is centred and shows a count only when steps wait.
- **Fixed:** On a phone, the browser's top and bottom bars take the colour of an open dialog or full-screen page, and go back to the studio's colour when it closes, including when one dialog opens over another.
- **Fixed:** On a computer with a mouse, the header art in the sidebar reaches the very top of the window; the thin colour bands meant for a phone's bars no longer show there.
- **Fixed:** A plugin's page now opens at its top, with its name, version and on/off switch in view, instead of at the list's scroll position. On a phone, Settings cards stand out from the page in light themes, the sidebar's six footer icons sit in an even grid, and the stats under the message box drop their second reading rather than cutting words in half. The message box shows a short prompt, token counts no longer print a floating decimal point, Appearance tiles and buttons no longer cut their labels, and plugin rows read in plain words with their identifiers kept in a tooltip.
- **Fixed:** The sidebar's Working group now stays at the bottom, directly above the foot icons, however few chats the list holds, and its last chat is no longer dimmed.

## Chats and workspaces
- A chat with a live agent that you switched on in preview 1.16 or 1.17 loses its place when you update: it shows no earlier limit or ended notice, and its next turn sends the agent every earlier message of the chat again. Chats written by this version are refused by those two previews.
- In a question card, the "Recommended" chip sits at the right end of the option's first line, so the option's text and description fill the space to its left.

## Safety and privacy
- Phones confirm sensitive actions with Face ID, or with the device lock where there is no Face ID, and set it up when pairing or in Settings, Devices. It is on for every home, with no preview switch to turn on; a home that had set the old `unlock.passkeys` preview switch keeps its other preview switches, and the retired one is ignored with a line in the log. A phone with no passkey confirms with its PIN. A sensitive action you take on a phone asks at the moment you take it, in plain words such as "Install a plugin", "Open a terminal" or "Save a model key": installing, updating, switching or removing plugins; full access, network settings, MCP server and external agent answers and approval answers; a terminal you open or look at, which asks once and again only after its confirmation lapsed (five minutes without a new terminal call, or an hour); a reload or a second tab on the same phone showing that terminal does not ask before then, and another terminal asks for itself; saved terminal tabs you are not looking at never ask, and a phone does not keep them alive, so the computer closes an idle one after two hours, as it does any idle terminal no window holds; the folder picker, which asks once while it stays open; new workspaces and their defaults, a chat in a folder outside your workspaces, slash commands, settings changes, saving or removing a model key, deleting from the trash forever, the details of a problem report, checking an external agent's plan limits now, and Changes and updates. One sheet shows at a time, and the next waits until you answer it. Cancel sends nothing, and the screen shows that the action did not go through. Some things stay at your computer and never ask on a phone: model-written plugins, the list of an agent's terminals in a chat's header, and browsing with your own browser while you are away. In the first minutes after a phone is paired, a change to a consent or guard plugin's settings waits until that setup window ends. Your computer never asks. Phone powers is gone, so there is no level to choose.
- One Face ID unlocks a phone for a while, so the next strong actions on it don't ask again. Settings, Face ID, Ask again after chooses how long: Every time, 5, 15, 30 or 60 minutes, 15 by default. The time counts from that Face ID and doesn't stretch with use. While it runs, the phone shows "Unlocked · 14 min" above the open chat with a Lock now button, and the count goes down until the chip disappears. Adding or removing a device or a passkey, the Face ID settings (the list of your devices' passkeys and Ask again after itself), turning protection off, turning full access on or off for a workspace or allowing a chat's request for it, and remote access still ask every time, with Face ID; what stays at your computer stays there. The time ends early when you tap Lock now, when the studio has been hidden for five minutes (checked again when you come back, so a phone that paused the page still locks, and one hidden tab locks the phone's other tabs too), when the phone has sent Delorun nothing for five minutes, when Ask again after changes, when the phone signs out or is removed, and when Delorun restarts. Only Face ID starts it: your PIN, a code from your computer and the first minutes after pairing never do, so a phone with no Face ID still types its PIN once for each action. Every time keeps one Face ID for each action. Changing Ask again after on a phone asks for Face ID itself; at your computer it changes at once. A deployment sets its own default with the home-auth plugin's `faceIdPassMinutes` setting (0, 5, 15, 30 or 60).
- Settings pages read more plainly. External agents shows its level choices as radio rows with the name on one line, and its switches stay beside their text on a phone. Network shows each workspace's folder as a short path from your home folder and puts the technical detail of its warning behind More. Devices keeps its safety note to one sentence. Changes lines up its headings and empty lines, shows Safe mode as a card like the others, and no longer prints a command. Setup versions has a shorter example name, and Face ID no longer shows its title twice on a phone.
- Every sensitive action on a phone now confirms in one bottom sheet that shows the action's own icon and title, the workspace and chat it belongs to when known, what it allows as you, what the confirmation covers and what stays afterwards. Confirm is a full-width button with the Face ID icon inside it, or "Confirm with PIN" on a phone with no passkey, which opens the keypad inside the same sheet. Cancel is a plain button, and nothing has a focus ring when the sheet opens.
- **Fixed:** Inside a chat sandbox, a `delorun` command that needs Delorun's folder now says the folder is hidden there and to run the command in the studio's terminal or from a chat with full access, instead of telling you to `chmod` a folder that is already private. `delorun plugin new` and `delorun plugin check` do not need the folder and run in the sandbox.
- **Fixed:** In Settings → Devices, the Approval window, Request expiry and Waiting requests fields are readable and editable again: each has its title and description in full beside a short number field, instead of one word per line behind the box.
- **Removed:** Phone powers, with its Careful and Full levels, is gone. A phone no longer has a level to choose: each action that changes what Delorun runs, such as opening a terminal, applying a change, turning protection off, making your copy of Delorun, restarting it and updating it, asks for Face ID, or for your PIN on a phone with no Face ID. With Face ID, one confirmation can cover the next few minutes of strong actions on that phone (Settings, Face ID, Ask again after; Every time asks for each one); the PIN always covers one action. A phone without Face ID can now do these strong actions, and installs, full access, network settings, MCP server and external agent answers, the folder picker, new workspaces, settings changes, saving or removing a model key and approval answers too, by typing its PIN once for each action: the PIN confirms that one action and opens no window, so the next action asks again, where Careful refused them before. A phone with Face ID confirms with Face ID, and that Face ID lets strong actions on that phone go without asking again for the time Ask again after names, 15 minutes unless you choose otherwise, except adding or removing devices and passkeys, the Face ID settings, turning protection off, a workspace's full access and remote access, which ask every time. No PIN unlock, code from your computer, approval grace or Never ask mode opens any of them, so a phone that was unlocked before still asks, and a refused action no longer asks for a code from your computer. Typing your PIN no longer unlocks a phone's page for a while: the PIN lock screen is gone, every phone is refused a PIN unlock, and a phone with no Face ID reaches exactly what a phone with Face ID reaches. A phone's Settings, Devices no longer offers Unlock, Show the code, Lock now or Add a device (it says to add devices at your computer), your computer's Settings, Devices shows an older unlock request only to decline it, `delorun reach on` and setup no longer say a phone cannot grant full access, PIN mode drops its idle time and lock-when-hidden choices (`delorun devices pin options` says they have no effect), and on a phone the first-run guide says model keys are tested at your computer instead of offering a Try again that always fails. A phone with no Face ID on a home with no PIN is told how to set up Face ID, or to set a PIN at your computer, instead of being shown a keypad that cannot work. `delorun devices powers` and `delorun setup --phone-powers` no longer exist, setup stops asking what an unlocked phone may do, the Settings, Devices choice is removed, and the `phonePowersOnUpgrade` setting of the device sign-in plugin and the `/auth/phone-powers` address are gone. An answers file that still holds `phonePowers` loads, and the answer is ignored with a line saying so.

## Plugins
- The Delorun studio bundle's page in Plugins says in a few plain lines what it adds and how to go back to the stock look, in place of the developer notes, and the Delorun local integration card describes itself in one sentence.
- **Fixed:** `delorun plugin new` now writes `engines.delorun` as `^0.1.0-0`, so `delorun plugin check` and the installer admit the plugin on a prerelease such as 0.1.0-preview.1.18 as well as on the final release, and `--dir` creates missing parent folders such as `plugins/home`.
