# Delorun 0.1.0-preview.2

## Install and update
- Delorun is an agent harness that you shape to your own work: the themes, the sidebar, the plugins, the modes, even Delorun itself. It is built on DeepSeek Harness, runs its plugins and imports your chats, and adds a studio you open in a browser, a guided first run, undo for every change to your setup, protections for what agents may do, and reaching your home from your phone. This first public version, 0.1.0-preview.2, is described below: everything that differs from stock DeepSeek Harness, grouped by where you meet it.
- Run it with `npx delorun`. The first run checks the download against the signed release manifest and the public release log, then guides you through setup. Delorun runs on Linux and on Windows through WSL2.
- This is a preview. Releases are signed with preview keys through the 0.x versions, and no outside security review has happened yet; [What you trust when you use Delorun](https://github.com/Delo-Run/delorun/blob/main/delorun/docs/guide/trust.md) says what that means. The rough edges you may meet are on the [known issues](https://github.com/Delo-Run/delorun/blob/main/delorun/docs/guide/known-issues.md) page, each with what to do.
- After an update, Settings → Updates offers **Go back…**, which returns to the release before it. It says that chats made since the update are set aside, not deleted, and can be removed later in Settings → Trash; when the update took no copy of your chats it offers **Go back, settings only…** and your chats stay as they are. You choose whether running chats are stopped now, and Delorun restarts on the earlier release. It needs the computer; a phone confirms with Face ID or its PIN first, and where a device may not do it the button reads "Update at your computer".

## Studio
- Multiview shows the agents a chat works with beside the main chat, in panes you can resize and rearrange, so you can watch several at once. Pin an agent from the agents list in the chat header; a pinned agent keeps its pane, with its name, whether it is working, waiting for you or done, and, for Claude Code and Codex, the plan and how much of its limits it has used. Press the Multiview button to open the panes and press it again to go back to the normal chat. When the chat hands work to a new agent, Multiview opens by itself and pins it; you can turn that off in the Multiview settings.
- Each pane is a real chat: you can approve or reject what an agent asks, reply to it and stop it without leaving Multiview, and Stop in one pane stops only that agent. Open full chat takes an agent into the main view, and Close removes its pane. A pane that is waiting for you says so, also when it is tabbed, left out for lack of room, or about to be closed.
- The layout starts with the main chat on the left and the agents stacked on the right. Drag a pane by its handle to split another pane or to join its tabs, use the Layout menu for Main and column, Side by side, Grid or Main on top, or choose Reset to default; none of these closes a pane. Each chat remembers its panes and layout, and a chat that had Multiview open shows it again when you come back. Up to six panes fit, main chat included, fewer on a small window.
- On a phone, or any window narrower than 768 pixels, Multiview becomes pages you swipe sideways: the main chat first, then each agent. A strip of names at the top jumps to a page, an amber dot marks an agent that is waiting for you, and the dots above the reply box show where you are.
- The Multiview settings let you turn off opening on every hand-off, change the pane limit, show the full reply box with its model and permission controls in each pane instead of the short one, and stop a chat reopening Multiview when you return to it.

## Safety and privacy
- `delorun setup prompts` offers a third answer, **Never ask (dangerous)**, for people who want Claude Code's bypass mode: any chat can then switch to full access with no question. It shows a warning, asks a yes-or-no that starts on No, and then asks you to type `full access everywhere`. A scripted setup needs `--prompts dangerous --accept-dangerous-mode`, `--yes` alone never selects it, and Delorun refuses it when it runs as root. While it is on, `delorun` at start, `delorun status` and `delorun doctor` say so, and choosing another answer turns it off.

## Reach
- **Security:** Asking the home which devices you trust, or removing one, works only on the home's own computer. A page opened from another address can no longer read your device list or remove a device, even when its name happens to reach the home, and a removal has to come from the studio itself rather than from another site.
