- The studio opens in Delorun's look: a softer palette with a choice of accents and themes, Delorun's typefaces, a density and a contrast setting, under Settings → Appearance, and a Conversation page for how chats are displayed. One switch in Settings → General → Look turns every Delorun addition to the studio off and shows the stock DeepSeek Harness look; with the stock look, one row under Settings → General turns the Delorun look back on.
- A profile set up with an earlier Delorun turns to the Delorun look at its next start; one switch in Settings → General → Look turns it back to the stock look.
- Under the Delorun look, a new chat's empty screen shows the Delorun wordmark above its headline.
- Under the Delorun look, the page shown while the studio loads names Delorun instead of HARNESS.
