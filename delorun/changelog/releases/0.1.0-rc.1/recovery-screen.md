- When the studio cannot start because a plugin fails to load or the page does not open within 30 seconds, and always at `/recovery`, a recovery screen shows what failed and your recent changes, and lets you turn off a plugin, undo a change or start in safe mode, in English or Chinese. These actions work only in a browser on the computer Delorun runs on; a remote session can read the screen and is told "This needs you at your computer."
