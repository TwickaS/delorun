- Every `delorun` command answers `--help` (and `-h`, and `delorun help <command>`) with a short usage, its options and its exit codes, and exits 0 without starting anything. A wrong option now says which option and points to `delorun <command> --help`, and `delorun help` ends with a short list of the words the commands use: home, profile, studio, workspace, full access and reach.
