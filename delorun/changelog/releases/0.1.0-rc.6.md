# Delorun 0.1.0-rc.6

## Studio
- Settings → Appearance → Branding lets you hide the Delorun mark and give the studio your own name, icon (picked or uploaded) and greeting. The sign-in and pairing pages show your name and an uploaded icon. The empty chat screen now shows the icon above the name.
- Two themes carry Delorun's own names, Slate and Berry, in place of the names of other products. A theme you saved before keeps working under its new name.
- The Look step of setup uses the same theme, accent and colour-scheme controls as Settings → Appearance, with a one-line explanation. The original DeepSeek Harness look is a quiet line under the choices; choosing it asks first and says that it closes the guide.
- The Phone access step of setup now reads as one sentence and one command (`delorun reach on tailscale`). The Tailscale checks, the approval command and the PIN mode option sit under **More**. A studio opened from a phone no longer shows the step.
- Setup now has a Models step right after Welcome. It lists the providers you can connect with a key, ticks the ones whose key it already found, walks you through each ticked provider (paste the key, see it tested, then Next or Skip) and ends with a summary. Each provider links to its own key page, and a pasted key is tested with the provider's free model list, which shows how many models it offers and the default one. Keys are write-only: Delorun shows "Key found" or "Key saved", never the key.
- In setup, **Skip** now leaves only the step you are on and moves to the next one. A quiet **Finish later** beside the step name closes the guide and keeps your place, so it opens again where you left off next time.
- Setup keeps the step name and "Step n of 7" with a progress bar at the top, and Skip, Back and Continue at the bottom, at every window width; only the middle scrolls. The list of steps at the side is gone. Welcome is the language plus a short preview note with "Read more". Agents, Recommended plugins and Models share one card: an icon (a neutral one where a plugin has none), a title, a one-line summary with "More" for the rest, and "Add" or "✓ Added". Errors and warnings show in a full-width line under the card with what to do next, and a failed plugin check shows a note at the top of the step.
- In setup, the Workspace step shows the chosen folder as a card with its name and path, and says what is happening in plain status lines. Ready lists what you set up in one calm colour: green no longer marks values, and "None added" is neutral.
- On mobile Safari the status bar and bottom toolbar take the studio's colours, and the sign-in page's, and follow a change of theme, accent or light and dark.
- **Fixed:** Settings → Appearance uses the same theme, accent and studio look chips as the first-run Look step, each a 44px target with keyboard support. The accent chips are locked, with a note, while a theme other than Soft is chosen, because a change would be saved without showing.
- **Fixed:** On a phone, "Run setup again" in Settings closes the sidebar as well, so it no longer stays open under the guide or beside the chat once the guide closes.

## Reach
- An approved phone can add and test model keys; stored keys are never shown, not even their first or last characters or their length, and a phone can name only the keys that model providers use.
- An approved phone can change everyday settings (appearance, accent, studio look, language, sidebar, onboarding progress) and turn on Delorun's own optional plugins, with no unlock; installing or removing plugins stays at the computer.
