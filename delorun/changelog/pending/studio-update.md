- **Update now** in the studio updates Delorun without a terminal. It shows the same report as `delorun update` first: what the new release turns off or removes, the changes you made to Delorun itself, whether your chats change format and the copy of them it takes by default. It asks you to accept each change that turns something off or removes it, waits for running chats or stops them as you choose, and reloads the page once the new release answers. Update now needs the computer, or under Phone powers Full a phone with an unlock you entered on it; a phone on Careful shows "Update at your computer".
- If the new release does not start well, Delorun goes back to the one you had on its own, and the studio says which check failed and that your chats are unchanged.
- **Go back** in Settings → General → Updates returns to the release before the last update with the same table of what happens to your chats and settings as `delorun recover rollback`, from the computer or under Phone powers Full an unlocked phone.
- **Stop Delorun** in the studio stops it, from the computer or an unlocked phone, and says that nothing in the studio can start it again: use the app icon or the launcher.
