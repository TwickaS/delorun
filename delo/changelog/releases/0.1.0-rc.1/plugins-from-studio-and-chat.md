- Settings, Plugins has Create a plugin: name a plugin, choose what it adds and a workspace, and Delo writes the starting files into the workspace's `plugins` folder, the same files `delorun plugin new` writes. Turn on lists the plugin in Changes for your review at the computer, also in dangerous mode; a phone or another device is told to do this at your computer.
- Ask the agent in a chat for a plugin and it makes one in the chat's workspace, under `plugins/<name>`, with the new `delo-plugin-authoring` skill. It cannot turn the plugin on, at any protection level and in any chat access; that stays your step at the computer. Once the plugin is on, later edits to its files wait for your review when changes protection is on, and reload live, logged as loaded without review, when it is off, the default.
