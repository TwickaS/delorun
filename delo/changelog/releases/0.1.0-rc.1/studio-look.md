- A profile set up with an earlier Delo turns to the Delo look at its next start; one switch in Settings → General → Look turns it back to the stock look.
- Under the Delo look, a new chat's empty screen shows the Delo wordmark above its headline.
- Under the Delo look, the page shown while the studio loads names Delo instead of HARNESS.
