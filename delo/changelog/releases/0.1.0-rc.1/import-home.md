- `delorun import-home` copies your DeepSeek Harness home, `~/.dsh` or the folder `--from` names, into your Delo home. It only reads `~/.dsh`, which stays exactly as it was. After one yes it copies your chats, exactly as they were saved, the images and files they refer to, and your workspace list.
- Then it asks about each of these on its own, and each starts on no: your profiles, your settings, your `AGENTS.md` instructions, your skills and your DeepSeek key. It shows each before it asks: each profile's plugins as carried, to install, added or not carried, the exact settings that would land, the first lines of your instructions, each skill's description, and your stored keys by name and never by value. `--dry-run` shows the preview and writes nothing.
- Only settings that change how Delo looks and your default model by name carry over: your theme and fonts, how chats and the composer show things, and your language. Every other setting is held back even after a yes, because it could run code, send your requests or key elsewhere, or switch off a protection; the preview lists each with the reason, and the import writes them where nothing reads them. A profile lands with its plugin list alone: no install script, lockfile, build approval or plugin from a path. A profile named `headless` stays behind, because `delorun headless` sets up its own, and so does a profile whose name Delo can't start, such as one with a capital letter or a dot.
- Setup asks "Copy your DeepSeek Harness chats, attachments and workspace list?" after its preview when `~/.dsh` exists and your Delo home has no chats yet, starting on no, and copies only those. `delorun import-home` copies the rest.
- The preview lists every workspace with its folder. A workspace in your home folder itself, a hidden folder of it such as `.ssh`, the root of your disk or a Harness home stays behind with its chats, and so does a chat that works outside its workspace's folder or stopped in the middle of a turn. Before each question the import shows the whole text that would land, a page at a time.
- A chat that ran with full access in DeepSeek Harness opens on the safer preset until you allow full access for its workspace in Delo.
- The import refuses a Delo home that already holds chats unless you add `--merge`, and never overwrites a chat, attachment or workspace. Links, pipes, other drives, files linked to something outside `~/.dsh`, attachments whose content does not match their name, and names DeepSeek Harness does not write are never followed or copied; no plugin, script or install runs; it copies at most 4 GiB unless `--max-size` gives more; and if your computer stops during an import, the next start of Delo removes the part it had copied.
- After the import, `delorun plugin --profile <name> install` installs each profile's plugins; the import prints the command for every profile that needs it, and a plugin's install script runs only after you approve it.
