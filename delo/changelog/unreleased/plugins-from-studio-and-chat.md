- Settings, Plugins has Create a plugin: name a plugin, choose what it adds and a workspace, and Delo writes the starting files into the workspace's `plugins` folder, the same files `delorun plugin new` writes. Turn on lists the plugin in Changes for your review at the computer, also in dangerous mode; a phone or another device is told to do this at your computer.
- Ask the agent in a chat for a plugin and it makes one in the chat's workspace, under `plugins/<name>`, with the new `delo-plugin-authoring` skill. It cannot turn the plugin on; that stays your step at the computer, and with changes protection on, later edits to the plugin's files wait for your review before they load.
