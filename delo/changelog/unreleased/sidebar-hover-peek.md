- In the studio's collapsed sidebar, moving the pointer to the rail — or to the left edge where the desktop hides the rail — opens the full sidebar over the page, without moving what you are reading and without pinning it open. It closes again when the pointer leaves, on Escape, or when keyboard focus leaves it, and hovering one of the rail's own controls never opens it, so every rail click still does what it did.
