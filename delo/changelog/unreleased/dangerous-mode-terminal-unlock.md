area: Safety and privacy
section: Changed
---

- A terminal opened or attached from a phone or browser always needs that device unlocked: setup's Dangerous answer, a full-access workspace and the old **Terminal from phone without unlock** switch no longer waive the unlock. The switch is gone from Settings, Devices; a home that still has it set keeps working and ignores the old setting. A phone with a terminal, or prompting a chat in a full-access workspace, has the computer's authority, so treat an unlocked phone as the computer.
