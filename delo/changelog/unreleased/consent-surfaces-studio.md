- Picking Full access in a chat whose folder has no full access yet now shows what it means before anything changes: the chat's commands could run outside the sandbox and write anywhere on the computer, read your files, Delo's home with its keys and tokens, and reach the Docker supervisor and Delo's update channel, for that folder only. Turn on full access records your answer and switches the chat; Cancel leaves it in the sandbox.
- Settings, Workspace access lists each folder with its full access and since when, and turns it on, with the same explanation, or off again; turning it off puts that folder's open chats back on the safer permission at once.
- Settings, MCP servers lists each local MCP server that Delo held because its command is new or changed, with its command, arguments, working folder and the names of its environment variables without their values. Accept, after a confirmation, starts it without restarting Delo; Decline keeps it stopped.
- These answers are given only on the home's own computer. On a phone or another device the sections and the full-access explanation say so and offer no button. With "Same as stock" prompts the sections say the profile does not ask, and the permission picker switches as DeepSeek Harness does.
