- The sidebar lists chats as cards with their status, sorted with pinned chats first, with search, a workspace filter, Alt and a digit to jump to one of the first nine chats, and an undo for each change you make from the list. Each workspace can have its own icon (letters, a glyph, an emoji or a generated pattern, in its own colour and shape), and the top of the sidebar can show header art; both are chosen under Settings → Sidebar.
