- A Changes section in Settings lists the changes to your plugins that wait for your review, the changes a failed start left out, your agent's plugin requests and your recent changes. Review shows a change's folder, its files, a summary of what it can do with the reminder that the summary helps you read the change and is not a protection, the files the diff does not show, and the diff; Apply asks a second time and names what the summary found when it flagged anything. Discard, Undo and Apply again work from the same section, Start working loads a plugin's edits live, and Done shows the folder's version with its diff and approves exactly that version. The section shows the protection setting with the terminal command that changes it, and your changes to Delo itself with their diff and the terminal command that decides each. While a work session lasts, a badge at the foot of the sidebar names the plugin. A browser that is not on the computer Delo runs on sees everything with every action disabled and "This needs you at your computer."
