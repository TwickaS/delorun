- The first device to claim your Delo home becomes its owner, using the one-time code that `delorun setup` prints: the code works once, expires ten minutes after it was printed, and a freshly printed code replaces it. A home nobody has claimed says so when you ask for its devices, instead of acting as if it had none.
- A home lists the devices you have trusted, with when each last signed in and where it is trusted, and removing one signs it out of every open session before the removal finishes, so nothing keeps answering in its name.
