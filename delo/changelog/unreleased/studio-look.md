- The studio opens in Delo's look: a softer palette with a choice of accents and themes, Delo's typefaces, a density and a contrast setting, and a Conversation section for how chats are displayed, all under Settings → Appearance. One switch at the top of Appearance turns every Delo addition to the studio off and shows the stock DeepSeek Harness look; with the stock look, one row under Settings → General turns the Delo look back on.
- A profile set up with an earlier Delo turns to the Delo look at its next start; one switch under Settings → Appearance turns it back to the stock look.
- Under the Delo look, a new chat's empty screen shows the Delo wordmark above its headline.
