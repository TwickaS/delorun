- A chat can be snoozed until later or settled when it is done, from its menu or its card; it moves to a folded Snoozed or Settled shelf at the bottom of the chat list and comes back when its snooze ends or when you send it a message. The snooze times and an optional rule that settles chats left idle for some days are set under Settings → Sidebar. The states are kept on your computer and never reach the model.
